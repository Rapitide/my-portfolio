Why I Keep Making Small Things

Sep 7, 2026

A finished small thing teaches me more than a perfect plan that never becomes real. Once an idea exists, even in a rough form, it can answer questions that thinking alone cannot.

I try to make the smallest version that can be used, observed, and changed. The result is rarely the final shape, but it gives the next decision something solid to stand on.

That is how I want to keep learning: by making, noticing, and making again.

Building MeLink at Saitama University

Sep 7, 2026

These days I am developing MeLink, an app for students at Saitama University. It is a chance to work on something close to my everyday life and the people around me.

Building for a familiar community makes small details matter. A short conversation, a confusing screen, or a useful shortcut can all change what the product should become.

I am still learning what MeLink can be, one release and one observation at a time.

A Wide Curiosity

Sep 7, 2026

I like moving between language, psychology, software, games, music, video, and 3D graphics. They look like different subjects, but each one changes how I notice people, systems, and possibilities.

Changing mediums is a useful way to change my thinking. A problem that feels abstract in code can become clear through an image, a sound, or a conversation.

I want to keep that curiosity wide enough for unexpected connections to appear.
