Some things I believe:
  • Start by doing the thing
    • An idea becomes clearer after it exists.
    • Make a small version before trying to make the perfect version.
    • Let experiments do some of the thinking.
  • Keep curiosity wide
    • Language, code, and creative tools can belong in the same conversation.
    • A change of medium can reveal a different way to think.
  • Make things that feel honest
    • Useful work should still feel like it came from a person.
    • The things I make do not need to be perfect to be worth making.
